Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Sant Baba Moni Sahib Vriddhanand Ashram respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, what information may be collected when you use our website and how it may be used.

1. Information You Provide

If you voluntarily contact the Ashram or use a form on this website, you may provide information such as your name, phone number, age, message, or other details necessary to respond to your request.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless it is specifically required for an Ashram service and you are comfortable providing it.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, process requests, coordinate volunteer or Ashram-related activities, and communicate with you about the request you submitted.

We do not state that a form submission creates an automatic admission, registration, employment, internship, or other guaranteed service. Requests may require separate verification by the Ashram.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use browser storage or cookies for basic website functionality. For example, the language-selection experience may use session storage so that a visitor's language choice is remembered during a browsing session.

4. Advertising

This website may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to applicable laws, user choices, and Google's policies.

For more information about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services, visit Google's Partner Sites policy. Visitors may also manage available advertising personalization choices through Google Ads Settings.

5. Third-Party Services and Links

The website may contain links to third-party services such as Google Maps or other external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. Please review the privacy policies of those services before providing information to them.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable care with information received through this website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact the Ashram so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website functionality, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
