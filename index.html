About Sant Baba Moni Saheb Vriddh Anand Ashram

Sant Baba Moni Saheb Vriddh Anand Ashram in Akurdi is a Vriddha Ashram serving elderly people and also a place of Langar Seva. The Ashram operates every day and provides food to people who come to its door, while caring for elderly residents.

Food is prepared at the Ashram twice a day to support the meals served during the day. In addition to food prepared at the Ashram, some food is received through larger Langars and food donations.

The Ashram's work depends on continued support, service and participation from people who wish to help.

Langar Seva

Langar is an important part of the Ashram's daily service. Food is cooked at the Ashram twice a day, helping provide the day's meals. People who come to the Ashram are welcomed for food, not only the elderly residents.

The food service is also supported by contributions of prepared food from larger Langars and by food donations. This shared effort helps the Ashram continue its daily service.
